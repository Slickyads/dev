## What Otterly Does
Otterly tracks AI engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Claude) to see:
- Which brands get mentioned in AI answers
- Brand ranking, sentiment, coverage
- Citation sources — which URLs AI pulls from
- Intent volume per prompt

It's a GEO visibility tool — replaces manual Perplexity tracking with automated daily/weekly scans.
