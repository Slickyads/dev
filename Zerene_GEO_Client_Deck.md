## Slide 3 — How to Measure GEO Rank & SOV
**GEO Rank** = Position in AI-generated answers across:
- Perplexity AI, ChatGPT Search, Google AI Overviews, Claude
- Measured by prompt cluster: category intent, brand direct, problem-solution, competitor comparison

**SOV (Share of Voice)** = Brand mentions / Total mentions in category prompts
- Zerene SoV = 3/15 = 20%
- Target: 60%+ within 6 months (industry benchmark for niche luxury)

**Sentiment** = Qualitative tone of AI responses
- When Zerene *is* mentioned → positive (4.6/5 Trustpilot cited, "premium French oils")
- Gap: AI never surfaces Zerene for *discovery* → zero sentiment opportunity

**Tracking Method:** Weekly Perplexity audit across 15 prompts (English + Arabic, KSA/UAE/GCC)

## Slide 7 — How to Rank in GEO (The Formula)
**Entity Clustering** — Every page must co-occur:
`[Target Audience] + [Use-Case Intent] + [Product Spec/Safety] + [Regional Location]`

Example: "VIP Corporate Clients" + "Ramadan Gifting" + "200ml Alcohol-Free Oud Spray" + "Riyadh, KSA"

**Q&A Format** — Structure content as direct answers AI can extract:
- H2 header = exact user prompt
- First 40 words = standalone factual summary
- Data table = Price, MOQ, Longevity, Fulfillment
- FAQ accordion = 3-5 procurement questions

**Off-Site Signals** — AI cross-validates with third-party citations:
- GCC luxury publication features
- Directory listings (Savoir Flair, What's On Dubai)
- LinkedIn thought leadership on "corporate sensory architecture"

**Good SEO = Good GEO** — 80% of success is standard SEO (crawlable pages, clear architecture, helpful content). The 20% shift: AI also reads what *others* say about you.
