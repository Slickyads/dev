2. Why Should Customers Write These Prompts?

The goal is intent signaling: when real users (or AI evaluation) type these prompts, AI engines notice Zerene being mentioned or cited. Even if no one types them yet, adding them to your Core Baseline creates a:

- Measurement anchor — you track whether Zerene appears as you add content
- Competitive benchmark — you see which competitors appear for these queries
- Content gap identifier — if Zerene is missing, you know exactly what to build

▎ The logic: AI models synthesize answers by looking for co-occurring entities. If a page mentions "Zerene" + "200ml reed diffusers" + "Riyadh" + "corporate gifting", AI volume skyrockets. Prompts are the trigger that makes AI look for those entities.

C. How to Increase Intent Volume in Otterly

▎ Important: You cannot directly increase a prompt's intent volume — it is an estimate of how frequently that query intent is searched across AI engines, based on Google search volume (AI platforms don't publish their underlying query-usage data).

To target higher-volume opportunities:

1. Replace or add prompts that reflect real buyer questions — prioritize non-branded, middle-to-bottom-funnel questions that imply evaluation or purchase intent, rather than broad informational questions such as "What is X?"
2. Use prompt variations: Source relevant variations through:
   - Otterly's AI Prompt Research tool
   - Query Fan-Out (start with one prompt, generate related variants)
   - Google Search Console (see what queries already bring traffic)
   - Customer surveys and sales/support conversations
3. Prioritize prompts rated 4–5: In Otterly, the five-segment indicator ranges from very low (1) to very high (5). Levels 4–5 are the strongest candidates for GEO prioritization.
4. Keep lower-volume prompts when commercially relevant: Intent volume helps prioritize demand, while prompt monitoring measures your brand coverage and website citations for each exact prompt.

- Blue Ocean: "alcohol-free luxury room spray aesthetic clinic Riyadh," "customized executive co-branding Saudi Arabia" — narrower, high-value segment, less competition, higher quality justification.
