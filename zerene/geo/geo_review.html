Zerene GEO Strategy Review

Generative Engine Optimization Progress Report

Executive Summary

Over the past nine months, Zerene has established a strong strategic foundation for GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), focusing on aligning content with AI search behavior, improving technical SEO, and mapping B2B intent in key GCC markets. While cultural alignment and brand sentiment remain strong (100% likelihood to buy), critical execution gaps in product specifications, pricing transparency, and structured data have limited AI visibility to 20% Share of Voice across tracked prompts.

The core challenge identified is that AI search engines operate as data-driven shopping assistants requiring concrete facts (prices, volumes, specifications) - not just brand philosophy - to make recommendations. Without these elements, Zerene loses to competitors who provide clear procurement data despite weaker brand alignment.

Share of Voice (SoV) Formula

Otterly calculates: prompts mentioning Zerene ÷ all eligible prompts × 100. Start with Core Baseline (15–20 prompts). Report SoV alone, then show Exploratory separately. Minimum mention threshold: don't report NSS for prompts with <10 total mentions.

Team FAQ

Q: Why does Otterly show 0% for KSA category prompts?

Because the website has no pricing, specs, or KSA-specific signals. AI can't extract facts it doesn't have. Fix the on-page data first.

Q: Why is intent volume low for our segment?

Because we're targeting a narrow premium B2B ICP. Lower volume is expected — track trends, not absolute numbers.

Q: Should we target broad "corporate gifts KSA" prompts?

No — these are red ocean, low relevance to premium ICP. Focus on blue-ocean Tier 3/4 prompts instead.

Q: How many prompts should we track in Core Baseline?

Start with 15–20. Goal is a stable, fixed set for month-over-month comparison, not maximum count.

Q: What if a prompt generates neutral or negative sentiment?

Use prompt-level drill-down to identify why (missing price, wrong audience). Then improve the relevant content and remeasure the unchanged Core Baseline.

Q: We don't have KSA contacts — how do we show AI we're present?

Add a KSA address (even PO Box), list SAR pricing and 2–4 day shipping, create a /ksa page, get listed in local directories. These entity signals help AI associate the brand with KSA.

Q: How often should we report SoV?

Monthly, using the same Core Baseline. Report Core Baseline alone, then show Exploratory separately. Trend data is more useful than a single snapshot.
