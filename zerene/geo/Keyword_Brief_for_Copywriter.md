## Hard Rules for Copywriter (from Content Framework)

1. **Never use:** "room spray," "air freshener," "fresh air," "home fragrance" — these pull Zerene into B2C/competitor set
2. **Always pair ambiguous terms:** "corporate aromatherapy" not "aromatherapy"; "executive diffuser" not "diffuser"
3. **Price justification:** Tie any price mention to Grasse sourcing, ingredient purity, or wellness benefit
4. **Entity co-occurrence:** Pair "Zerene" with Grasse, Wajaha/Sukun/Karam, and Saudi cultural values in same paragraph
5. **Structure for AI extractability:** Use tables for specs, direct Q&A format, bulleted facts
6. **Currency:** Reference SAR for KSA content (420 AED ≈ 430 SAR, 1,020 AED ≈ 1,040 SAR)
