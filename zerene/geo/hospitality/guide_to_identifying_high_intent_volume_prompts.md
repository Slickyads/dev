# How to Identify Prompts with High Intent Volume

This guide outlines a structured methodology to discover, analyze, and validate prompts and search queries that possess both **high intent** (strong likelihood to convert) and **viable query volume**.

---

## 1. High-Intent Prompt Framework

To qualify a prompt as having high intent volume, evaluate it against three primary criteria:

$$\text{High Intent Volume} = \text{Search Demand (MSV)} \times \text{Commercial Modifiers} \times \text{SERP Monetization}$$

### A. Intent Category Classification

1. **Transactional / Action-Oriented (Highest Value):**
   * Prompts containing direct purchase terms: *"buy"*, *"order"*, *"procure"*, *"supplier"*, *"vendor"*, *"pricing"*.
   * *Example:* *"Where to buy corporate executive fragrance gifts in Jeddah?"*

2. **Commercial Investigation (High Value):**
   * Prompts comparing solutions or brands: *"vs"*, *"top luxury suppliers"*, *"best B2B platforms"*, *"reviews"*.
   * *Example:* *"Compare Zerene vs Aroma360 for luxury hotel scenting"*

3. **Informational / Advisory (Lower Direct Conversion):**
   * General learning prompts: *"how to"*, *"what is"*, *"benefits of"*.
   * *Example:* *"How to choose an essential oil diffuser"*

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Discovery Process

### Step 1: Analyze Search Engine & Tool Data (Metrics)

Use keyword research tools (e.g., Google Keyword Planner, Semrush, Ahrefs, or Google Trends) to measure real-world volume:

* **Monthly Search Volume (MSV):** Look for consistent or growing search query volume within your target geographic region (e.g., KSA, UAE, Global).
* **Cost Per Click (CPC):** High CPC values (e.g., $> \$5.00$) signify strong commercial intent because advertisers actively bid high amounts for those users.
* **Keyword Difficulty (KD):** Higher KD often correlates with lucrative, high-volume search terms where competitors invest heavily.

### Step 2: Look for High-Intent Modifiers

High-intent queries often combine specific business constraints. Screen your prompt lists for these three modifier layers:

| Modifier Layer | Purpose | Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Geo-Location** | Targets local conversion intent | *"in Riyadh"*, *"Jeddah"*, *"Saudi Arabia"* |
| **Budget / Spec Limits** | Pre-qualifies purchase power | *"under 1000 SAR"*, *"IFRA-compliant"*, *"bulk order"* |
| **Audience / B2B Qualifier** | Filters for enterprise decision-makers | *"for hotels"*, *"B2B suppliers"*, *"corporate gifting"* |

### Step 3: SERP & AI Engine Validation Signals

Inspect actual Search Engine Results Pages (SERPs) and AI answers to verify intent volume:

1. **Presence of Sponsored Ads & Shopping Cards:** 
   * If a prompt triggers multiple paid text ads or product carousels, it confirms that active commercial volume exists for that topic.
2. **Google Autocomplete & "People Also Ask" (PAA):**
   * Prompts that auto-complete or show up as PAA expansions represent high-frequency user searches.
3. **AI Search Citation Rates (GEO Signal):**
   * Prompts that generate multi-source vendor listings in AI engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) indicate established commercial demand and content indexation.

## 3. High vs. Low Intent Volume Comparison

| Parameter | High Intent Volume Query | Low Intent Volume Query |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prompt Example** | *"Top B2B luxury fragrance suppliers for corporate gifting in Riyadh"* | *"Why do perfumes smell good?"* |
| **User Profile** | Procurement Manager / Decision Maker | Casual Reader / Student |
| **Search Volume (MSV)** | Moderate–Low (Niche B2B) or High (B2C) | Very High |
| **CPC / Commercial Value** | High | Low |
| **Likely SERP Layout** | Local Map Pack, B2B Directories, Paid Ads | Wikipedia, Blogs, Informational Guides |

---

## 4. Evaluation Checklist for Prompts

When reviewing prompt datasets, score each prompt using this 4-point checklist:

- [ ] **Contains an Actionable Modifier?** (*"buy"*, *"supplier"*, *"compare"*, *"best"*)
- [ ] **Includes Regional or Audience Context?** (*"Saudi Arabia"*, *"corporate"*, *"hotels"*)
- [ ] **Triggers Paid Search or Local Business Listings?**
- [ ] **Targets a Specific Problem or Regulatory Gate?** (*"IFRA-compliant"*, *"ISO certified"*)

*A prompt meeting **3 or more** criteria is classified as a **High Intent** target.*
