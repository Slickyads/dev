Phase 1: Discovery & ICP Query Validation

Step 1

Define ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

  • Map exact B2B decision-maker personas (Procurement Heads, VIP Gifting Managers, Hospitality Directors).
  • Define regional intent signals (KSA, UAE, GCC) and cultural requirements (Ramadan, National Day).
  • Establish commercial tier priorities (Executive Gifting, Hotel Scenting, Spa/Wellness).
Step 2

AI Engine Query Audit

  • Test natural language prompts directly in ChatGPT Search, Perplexity AI, Claude, and Google AI Overviews.
  • Identify missing citations, competitor strongholds, and zero-click answer boxes.
  • Categorize queries by intent (Commercial, Informational, Comparison, Seasonal).
Slide 4 / 10

Phase 2: GEO Keyword Architecture & Fact-Dense Content

Step 3

Keyword Architecture & Targeting

  • Structure H2/H3 headers using verbatim long-tail conversational questions.
  • Target high-intent entity keywords (e.g., custom perfume suppliers Saudi Arabia).
  • Integrate parallel bilingual Arabic/English keyword matrices for regional GCC search.
Step 4

Fact-Dense Content Creation (CSQAF)

  • Apply CSQAF framework: Citations, Statistics, Quotes, Authoritativeness, and Fluency.
  • Place direct 40–60 word answer boxes at the top of informational sections for immediate LLM extraction.
  • Embed structured comparison tables, lists, and clinical study data.
Slide 5 / 10

Phase 3: Indexing Triggers & Off-Page Consensus Signals

Step 5

LLM Crawl Triggering

  • Implement JSON-LD Schema (Organization, Product, FAQPage).
  • Request manual re-indexing via Search Console's URL Inspection tool.
  • Maintain clear HTML hierarchy and eliminate blocking JavaScript snippet tags.
Step 6

Off-Page Consensus Building

  • AI models check external web consensus to verify brand authority before citing.
  • Publish press releases and B2B case studies on industry trade portals.
  • Secure co-mentions on GCC procurement directories and hospitality journals.
Slide 6 / 10

Phase 4: AI Citation Analysis & Rapid Refreshes

Step 7

Citation Audit & Diagnostic

  • Audit live AI responses to inspect footnote citation links (e.g., [1], [2]).
  • Identify missing data gaps: Why did an AI cite a competitor over your page?
  • Evaluate domain authority and entity association score per target prompt.
Step 8

Rapid 24-48h Content Refreshes

  • Update uncited pages with explicit procurement metrics (MOQs, delivery coverage, IFRA compliance).
  • Re-align page H1 headers verbatim with the target AI user prompt.
  • Resubmit updated URLs via Google Search Console and social signals for instant recrawl.
Slide 7 / 10

Phase 5: Automated Weekly AI Share of Voice (SoV)

Step 9

Automated SoV Tracking Engine

  • Deploy Python scripts utilizing Perplexity & OpenAI APIs to test prompt matrices weekly.
  • Calculate GEO Share of Voice: (Cites / Total Prompts) * 100.
  • Generate automated performance dashboards and strategic recommendations.

Mathematical GEO Score Formula

GEO Score = (Direct Link Citation × 40%)
+ (Brand Mention × 30%)
+ (Top 3 Rank Position × 30%)

Target Benchmark: Maintain >35% GSoV across core commercial prompt clusters.
Slide 8 / 10

Phase 6: Machine-Readable Knowledge Base Engine

Step 10

GEO FAQ & Knowledge Base Hub

  • Maintain a structured Q&A repository answering all long-tail Search Console queries.
  • Wrap all Q&A accordions with parallel JSON-LD FAQPage schema.
  • Provide clear, authoritative 40-word scientific and procurement answers.

Key Knowledge Base Components

  • Commercial: MOQs, Custom Branding, Regional Shipping.
  • Scientific: Active compounds, clinical studies, cortisol reduction stats.
  • Cultural: Saudi business etiquette, Taif Rose, Oud traditions.
Slide 9 / 10

Phase 7: Lead Conversion & ICP Data Capture

Step 11

Convert AI Citations into B2B Sales

  • Place explicit Call-To-Action (CTA) elements above the fold on all cited landing pages.
  • Implement seamless B2B quote request forms (e.g., zerene.com/pages/partnerships).
  • Track traffic arriving from Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, and AI Overviews via UTM tags.

The Conversion Loop

AI engines cite your authoritative content → High-intent B2B buyer lands on specialized page → Direct quote request generated → Pipeline revenue closed.
