Part 2: Master GEO & SEO Technical Audit Checklist

1. Technical & AI Accessibility

  • Verify JSON-LD FAQ Schema
    Ensure key Q&A sections include valid `FAQPage` schema so scrapers parse exact QA pairs.
    Critical
  • Audit Robots.txt & Meta Snippets
    Confirm no `nosnippet` or User-Agent rules block GPTBot, PerplexityBot, or Google-Extended.
    High
  • Server-Side Rendering (SSR)
    Ensure core text definitions exist in raw HTML without requiring heavy JS rendering.
    High

2. On-Page GEO Architecture

  • Verbatim Header Alignment
    Check if H1/H2 headers verbatim match target prompts (e.g., `

    How Does Rose Essential Oil Work?

    `).
    Critical
  • 40-Word Summary Answer Blocks
    Place a 2–3 sentence direct declarative summary box immediately beneath each H2 header.
    Critical
  • Fact Density & Statistical Data
    Include explicit metrics (MOQ 50 units, IFRA safety standards, clinical study numbers).
    High

3. Bilingual & Off-Page Validation

  • Bilingual HTML Direction (dir/lang)
    Wrap English in `dir="ltr"` and Arabic in `dir="rtl"` for clean font rendering and AI parsing.
    Critical
  • GCC Third-Party Directory Listings
    Ensure Zerene is listed on Saudi & UAE B2B procurement portals to build external web consensus.
    High
  • Above-the-Fold Lead CTAs
