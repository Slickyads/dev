4. Technical Best Practices for Single-Page Bilingual SEO & GEO

  • 1. HTML Language & Direction Attributes: Wrap English blocks in <div lang="en" dir="ltr"> and Arabic blocks in <div lang="ar" dir="rtl">. This ensures Google and AI scrapers process font rendering and text direction cleanly.
  • 2. Bilingual JSON-LD Schema: Include both language Q&A pairs inside your FAQPage schema markup. AI models will extract the English answer for English queries and the Arabic answer for Arabic queries from the exact same page URL.
  • 3. Hreflang Tags (If Separate Subdirectories Exist): If you also maintain dedicated subdirectories (e.g., /blogs/ and /ar/blogs/), include self-referencing hreflang="en" and hreflang="ar" link tags in the HTML header.
