1. Content Writer Briefing Workflow (The SOP)

Before any content writer begins drafting a new article or landing page for Zerene, they must receive a standardized Content & Keyword Brief. Follow this 4-step writing process to guarantee AI engine citations (ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews):

  • Step 1: Identify ICP & Intent Cluster: Define whether the piece targets B2B procurement (hotels, corporate gifting) or AI informational research (essential oil benefits, cultural etiquette).
  • Step 2: Assign Bilingual Keywords: Provide 2–3 exact primary keywords and prompts in both English and Arabic from the Master Bilingual Keyword Matrix.
  • Step 3: Enforce CSQAF Fact Density: Require the writer to include hard numbers (e.g., MOQ 50 units, clinical study statistics) and structured comparison tables.
  • Step 4: Mandatory 40-Word Answer Blocks: Require a concise 2–3 sentence summary box directly beneath every H2 question header for instant LLM extraction.
