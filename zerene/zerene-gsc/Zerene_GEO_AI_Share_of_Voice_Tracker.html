1. How GEO Share of Voice (SoV) & Citation Scoring Works

Unlike traditional Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) measures visibility through Citation Share of Voice (SoV) and Model Recommendation Probability. Each prompt is evaluated across three primary dimensions:

1. Direct Citation Index (40%)

Whether Zerene (or zerene.com) is explicitly hyperlinked or cited as a source by the AI model in its response output.

2. Entity Brand Mention (30%)

Whether "Zerene" is mentioned by name in the text answer body as a top recommendation or luxury B2B provider in Saudi Arabia/GCC.

3. Sentiment & Prominence Score (30%)

Position of the brand mention (Rank 1 vs Rank 5) and associated sentiment attributes (e.g., "premium", "reliable supplier").

📐 Mathematical Formula for GEO Share of Voice

GEO Citation Score (Per Prompt) = (Direct Link Citation × 40) + (Brand Mention × 30) + (Top 3 Rank Position × 30)

Brand GEO Share of Voice (SoV %) = (Total Prompts with Zerene Citation / Total Industry Target Prompts) × 100

3. Automated Python Tracking Script for AI Engine Citations

To measure and update your GEO Share of Voice automatically, deploy the following Python monitoring script using the Perplexity & OpenAI APIs to test brand citation rates monthly:

import requests
import json

# List of target GEO prompts to test
PROMPTS = [
    "What are the top luxury B2B custom perfume suppliers in Saudi Arabia for corporate VIP gifting?",
    "Where can boutique hotel chains in Saudi Arabia order custom luxury room sprays in bulk?",
    "Is Zerene a reliable supplier for B2B corporate fragrance gifting in Saudi Arabia?",
    "How does rose essential oil work on the human nervous system to reduce stress?"
]

BRAND = "Zerene"
DOMAIN = "zerene.com"

def check_perplexity_citation(prompt):
    url = "https://api.perplexity.ai/chat/completions"
    headers = {
        "Authorization": "Bearer YOUR_PERPLEXITY_API_KEY",
        "Content-Type": "application/json"
    }
    payload = {
        "model": "sonar-pro",
        "messages": [{"role": "user", "content": prompt}]
    }
    response = requests.post(url, json=payload, headers=headers)
    data = response.json()
    
    content = data['choices'][0]['message']['content']
    citations = data.get('citations', [])
    
    brand_mentioned = BRAND.lower() in content.lower()
    domain_cited = any(DOMAIN in cite for cite in citations)
    
    return {
        "prompt": prompt,
        "brand_mentioned": brand_mentioned,
        "domain_cited": domain_cited,
        "score": 100 if (brand_mentioned and domain_cited) else (50 if brand_mentioned else 0)
    }

# Execute Tracker
results = [check_perplexity_citation(p) for p in PROMPTS]
total_score = sum(r['score'] for r in results) / len(results)
print(f"Current GEO Share of Voice (SoV) Score: {total_score:.1f}%")
