## 1. Definition: Prompt Intent Volume

**Intent Volume** measures user search frequency/demand in AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, etc.).

| Property | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **What it measures** | How often a specific question or topic is asked to AI engines by real users |
| **What it reflects** | Buyer interest, search frequency, commercial readiness |
| **Key constraint** | You cannot force people to search a term more often — you observe and track existing organic demand |
| **Independence** | Exists independently of your brand; a river flowing regardless of your bucket |

### The River & Bucket Analogy

| Scenario | River (Intent Volume) | Bucket (Citations) | Strategic Meaning |
|----------|----------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Opportunity Gap** | High | Low/None | Buyers are asking; AI cites competitors/third parties — **PRIMARY TARGET** |
| **High ROI** | High | Strong | Maximum brand visibility & referral traffic — **DEFEND & EXPAND** |
| **Low Impact** | Low | Strong | You own the citation but nobody asks — **LOW PRIORITY** |

> **Goal:** Find 15 prompts where Zerene sits in **Opportunity Gap** or **High ROI** quadrants for KSA B2B intent.

## 3. Methodology: Finding High-Intent Prompts

### Phase 1: Seed from Ground-Level Intelligence (Internal)

| Source | Extraction Method |
|--------|-------------------|
| Sales call recordings | Tag questions prospects ask before buying |
| Support tickets / RFPs | Pull exact phrasing from vendor evaluation docs |
| Trade show / event conversations | Note language buyers use when describing needs |
| LinkedIn DMs / inbound inquiries | Capture raw question phrasing |
| Competitor win/loss analysis | What prompts drove decisions to competitors? |

### Phase 2: Reverse-Engineer from Traditional Search Data

1. **Export high-volume commercial keywords** (Ahrefs / SEMrush / Google Keyword Planner):
   - `VIP corporate gifting Saudi Arabia`
   - `luxury corporate gifts Riyadh`
   - `executive gifts Riyadh under 1000 SAR`
   - `corporate scenting solutions Riyadh`
   - `office fragrance suppliers Saudi Arabia`
   - `B2B fragrance procurement KSA`

2. **Fan-out into natural AI prompts** using this prompt in ChatGPT/Claude:

> **Turn these high-volume commercial keywords into 10 natural conversational prompts that a Procurement Director or HR VP in Saudi Arabia would ask an AI assistant when looking for vendor recommendations.**

3. **Map across buyer funnel** (4 archetypes per keyword cluster):

| Archetype | Template |
|-----------|----------|
| **Direct Recommendation** | "What are top-rated [category] suppliers in [city] delivering [spec] under [budget]?" |
| **Comparative / Evaluation** | "Best alternatives to [traditional approach] for [use case] in Saudi Arabia under [budget]." |
| **Use-Case / Constraint Specific** | "Where can I order [custom requirement] in [city] with [constraint: fast delivery / SAR pricing / VAT-compliant]?" |
| **Vendor Validation** | "Top-rated [category] companies in [city] for [specific buyer need: C-level / government / multicultural]." |

### Phase 3: Verify Citation Triggering (GEO Tools)

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Otterly.ai** | Track prompt → citation mapping; measure Zerene SoV per prompt |
| **DemandSphere / Brandlight** | Map prompt back to underlying commercial search volume |
| **Manual LLM testing** | Prompt each candidate in ChatGPT/Perplexity/Claude; record if citations appear |

**Pass criteria:** AI response includes ≥1 specific vendor citation (company name, product, or link).
