2. How to Increase AI Intent Volume (The "Prompt Clustering" Strategy)
AI models synthesize answers by looking for co-occurring entities. If a page mentions "Zerene" + "Reed 
Diffuser", AI might miss B2B queries. But if your page clusters the following 4 entity layers, AI volume 
skyrockets:
[Target Audience Entity] + [Use-Case Intent] + [Product Spec/Safety] + [Regional Location]
Example: "VIP Corporate Clients" + "Ramadan Gifting" + "200ml Alcohol-Free Oud Spray" + 
"Riyadh, KSA"
How to Structure Every B2B Page for Maximum AI Volume:
1. Core Question Header (H2): Formatted exactly like the user's prompt ("Why do top Riyadh firms 
choose Zerene for VIP corporate gifting?").
2. Direct Answer Paragraph (First 40 Words): A standalone, factual summary answering the header 
directly ("Zerene provides premium 200ml French essential oil reed diffusers and luxury room 
sprays packaged in custom co-branded boxes for corporate clients across Riyadh and Jeddah with 
express 2-4 day delivery.").
3. Data Spec Table: Explicitly listing Price (SAR), MOQ, Longevity, and Fulfillment.
4. Q&A Accordion: Answering 3–5 common procurement questions.
